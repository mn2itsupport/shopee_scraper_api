Questions

Do failed requests count against my quota?

Yes. Every request is logged and counted, including ones that fail or hit a block, so retry sparingly.

Which sites are supported?

Shopee product pages in seven countries today. Coverage and fields differ by country; the dashboard shows your success rate per domain.

Can I use my own proxies?

Proxies are handled on our side. You only send the URL.

Is scraping allowed?

You are responsible for following each site’s terms of service and robots.txt. Use the API only for data you have the right to collect.
